# A Weekend at the Cabin

## Getting There

The drive took about four hours. The last stretch was a gravel road that wound through the trees, and the cabin only came into view at the very end. It was smaller than the photos, but the view more than made up for it.

### What We Packed

Warm layers, a cast iron pan, too many books, and a deck of cards. The cards got the most use.

## The First Night

Once the sun went down, the temperature dropped fast. We lit the wood stove, made dinner, and sat by the window watching the last of the light fade off the mountains.

### Lessons Learned

Bring more firewood than you think you need. Bring a headlamp. And never assume the cabin has a working can opener.
